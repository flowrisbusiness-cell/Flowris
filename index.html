Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deliveries, customizations, and payments.

How long does delivery take inside and outside Dhaka? ▼

Inside Dhaka city, orders are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. For deliveries outside Dhaka (all 64 districts), parcels take 3 to 4 business days via Steadfast / Pathao / Sundarban courier services.

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) and bKash payment work? ▼

You can pay comfortably via Cash on Delivery upon receiving your package. For personalized orders (such as custom name engravings or photo prints), we may request a small advance booking fee of ৳200 via bKash / Nagad Send Money to confirm personalization materials.

Can you send the gift directly to the recipient with a handwritten note? ▼

Yes, absolutely! We love surprise gifts. Simply enter the recipient's delivery address and phone number during checkout, and write your secret message in the "Gift Note" box. We will hand-write your note on luxury calligraphy cardstock without attaching any invoice.

How long do preserved botanical flowers and resin art last? ▼

Epoxy resin keepsakes last a lifetime! Our flowers are dehydrated using Japanese botanical preservation techniques and sealed with anti-UV resin, protecting them against fading, humidity, and dust.
